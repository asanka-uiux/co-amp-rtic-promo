Questions

The details

Do I have to buy wheels to get the cooler?

No. A set of four qualifying AMP Tires on its own qualifies. Wheel and tire packages qualify as well, so either path gets you the cooler.

Which cooler do I get?

The Limited-Edition RTIC 32 QT Cooler in AMP Tires colors. It is only available through this promotion and not sold separately.

Is there a promo code?

No code needed. The cooler is applied automatically to qualifying orders placed during the promo window.

Does the promo work with financing?

Yes. Orders paid through Affirm or Katapult qualify the same as any other payment method.

How many coolers can I get?

One cooler per qualifying order, while supplies last.

Does the cooler ship with my tires?

No. The cooler is processed and shipped separately from your tires or wheel and tire package, and it arrives under its own tracking number.

Why is the cooler not on my invoice?

Free promotional coolers are handled outside your order, so they do not appear on your order confirmation, invoice, or packing slip. That is expected. If your order qualified, the cooler is on its way.

What if I return the tires?

If a qualifying order is returned or cancelled, the cooler must be returned as well, or its retail value will be deducted from the refund.
